# Interview Script v1

**Problem we are investigating:** how patients, especially those who live far from hospitals, experience travelling to hospitals, getting appointments, and waiting to see doctors, and how doctors experience working with large numbers of patients.

**Our prediction:** We are wrong if patients generally get appointments easily, reach the hospital without significant difficulty, see their doctor without long waits, and do not feel that the process takes too much of their time. We are also wrong if doctors generally do not experience large numbers of patients as stressful or disruptive to their work.

## Opening (2 min, word for word)

"I'm [name], a student at KIU, and this is my teammate [name]. We're doing a university project about how people deal with spending time at the hospitals when they need to as well as how doctors who actually work at those hospitals cope with the working environment. We're not selling anything and we have nothing to show you. We just want to hear what actually happened to you, so there are no wrong answers. Is it okay if my teammate takes notes? We won't use your name."

## Warm-up (2 min)
1. How often do you usually need to visit a doctor or hospital?

## Core (12 to 15 min)
2. Walk me through the last time you had to go to the doctor, from when you decided to go until you left the hospital.

3. How long did it take you to get to the hospital that day?

4. How long did it take you to wait in the waiting hall until you saw the doctor?

5. Tell me about the last time you had to wait a long time to see a doctor.

6. Tell me about the last time you had difficulty getting an appointment with a doctor.

7. What did you have to do while waiting to see the doctor?

8. What did the last hospital visit cost you in terms of your time or other things you had to give up?

9. Tell me about the busiest day you have experienced at a hospital.

10. **For doctors:** How do you generally feel about your job at the hospital?

11. **For doctors:** Walk me through the last day when you had a particularly large number of patients.

12. **For doctors:** Tell me about the last time the number of patients affected how you worked.

13. **For doctors:** How many patients do you usually have daily on average?

14. **For doctors:** What usually happens when more patients arrive than you expect?

15. **For doctors:** What part of your work becomes most difficult when there are many patients?

16. **For doctors:** Tell me about the last time many people arrived around the same time?

## Close (2 min)

17. Thinking about everything we've talked about, is there anything important about dealing with doctors or hospitals that I didn't ask about?

18. Who else should we talk to about this?

19. Could we come back when we have something to show you?

## Follow-up bank

- And then what happened?
- Why was that a problem?
- When was the last time that happened?
- What did you do?
- How long did it take?
- How many times did that happen?
- Can you show me?
