## Week 3 · Kill your favorite idea

My favorite idea was my own Step 1 belief: patients lose two to four hours waiting at the hospital before they can see their doctor. It was our first pick, and on 06.10 I kept it as the pick but narrowed it: the long wait is real, but it clusters on patients who come without an appointment after their calls go unanswered — with an appointment, Zura waits 20–30 minutes and calls it normal, and Gocha says his regular visits are fine. I tested my belief against every log that speaks to the wait instead of defending it. Logs 01 (grandmother), 02 (Gocha) and 03 (Zura) all hit a 1.5–2 hour wait on a difficult visit; log 05 (Beka's father) missed at 30 minutes, his pain being three days of calling, a wasted booking trip and a two-week wait for the slot; logs 04 (doctor) and 06 (aunt) confirmed the pressure from the doctor's side. Verdict: weakened — the wait is real, but it is a walk-in/failed-contact problem more than an every-visit problem. One more check came on 10.10: I asked the polyclinic receptionist (log 08), and she estimated booked patients wait about 10 minutes while patients without an appointment wait 40 minutes to an hour or more. That fits the walk-in caveat, and she remembered one specific case, a patient without an appointment who waited about 2.5 hours with about 10 patients ahead of her; the short call did not cover callbacks or call volume, so the reception-line guess in Pattern 2 stays open. Beyond my lane: all of this is discovery, not build. The hack: I converted the Gocha interview log and the interview script to the v2 layout, and filled the problem pool and hypothesis parts, in one evening so the team had one consistent format to score against instead of mixed drafts.

## Week 6 · The smallest complete journey

I did not write this entry in its own week; I am writing it on 27.11, from my Week 6 commits. I drew our MVP boundary as the smallest journey that still delivers the value: Gela sends his doctor what changed, the doctor answers, and Gela confirms it was resolved without a trip.
