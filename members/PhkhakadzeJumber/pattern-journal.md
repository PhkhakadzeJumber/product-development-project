# Pattern journal · Jumber Pkhakadze (@PhkhakadzeJumber)

Same prompt every week: Where did you apply this pattern this week, where did you exercise agency beyond your assigned lane, and what did you hack together to test something faster than the plan allowed? Link the DECISIONS.md lines you drove.

## Week 1 · Act like an owner, not a role

My lane is build: the repo and the code. I created the repo and the semester folder structure on 29.09, drafted the first team contract and the outreach tracker, and fixed the merge conflicts when three of us edited the contract at once. My three statements came from my sister's internship at the hospital in Kutaisi: patients waiting hours for their doctor, nurses retyping paper vitals at the end of the shift, and a friend who could not picture a cosmetic change. The waiting statement became the runner-up problem. Owner, not role: the repo was my job, but the hospital contacts came from me too, and that is where two of our interviews came from.

## Week 2 · Evidence over opinion

I asked in one interview and logged one. I interviewed Gocha on 05.10 in the hospital café after his check-up, introduced through my sister, with Luka and Beka logging. Going in I believed the problem was the hours of waiting. Gocha said the two-weekly visits are fine, "they know me", and the problem is the days in between: two calls, no call back, a tablet he cut in half himself. That is the opposite of my own statement, and it is in the log as it was said. I was second logger for Zura at his flat and counted the silence before Giorgi spoke after the half-tablet came out. My sister is passing a message to Gocha's neighbour on check-up days for the next interview.

DECISIONS.md lines I drove: the runner-up in the first line, hospital waiting, is my statement. The Gocha log is one of the four pieces of evidence behind the Week 3 verdict.
