## Week 2 · Evidence over opinion

I asked in one interview and logged one. I interviewed Zura at his flat on 05.10 with Beka and Jumber logging. The best thing I did was ask to see his phone: "many calls" became six exact dates in the call log, which is stronger than anything he said. The worst thing I did was ask "would it help if you could message her", which got "of course" and went into the log as red. I was the logger for Beka's grandmother and flagged the one moment Beka answered for her, so her "yes" is not counted but the details she gave herself are. Beyond my lane: both of those are discovery, not planning or code. Next: Zura is passing Dato's number to me, and I follow up.
