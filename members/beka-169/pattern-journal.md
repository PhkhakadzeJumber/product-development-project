## Week 1 · Act like an owner, not a role

My lane is discovery: interview quality and synthesis. I filled in my part of the contract and wrote three statements from things I had watched myself: my father walking out to the yard to check on a car, my cousin's flat arguing every month over who paid which bill, and my grandmother's monthly trip for the same prescription. The grandmother one is the one I had seen most often. It did not become the pick, but it is in the scorecard as the third shortlisted problem and it comes up in her log under question 1.

## Week 2 · Evidence over opinion

I asked in one interview and logged two. I interviewed my grandmother on 05.10 at her flat with Giorgi logging. She goes every month to renew the same prescription. On her last visit it took several tries to find an appointment that fit, the bus is about an hour each way, and she waited about two hours with an appointment before she had to leave without seeing the doctor. Giorgi watched for places where I answered for her. I was second logger for Gocha at the hospital café and for Zura at his flat, where I kept Giorgi on the calls when Zura jumped ahead to the trip, and made sure the six call-log dates were written down exactly. The thing I learned this week: having an appointment did not tell her when she would actually be seen, and she said that herself at the end, when I asked what I had missed.
