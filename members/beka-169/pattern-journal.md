## Week 2 · Evidence over opinion

I asked in one interview and logged two. I interviewed my grandmother on 05.10 at her flat with Giorgi logging. Her first answer was "I don't call, what for?", which on its own would have weakened the hypothesis. I waited, and the dizziness story came out on its own: one call, no answer, no number, pills stopped for a weekend. Giorgi flagged one question where I supplied the memory for her, and I agreed it goes in the log as red. I was second logger for Gocha at the hospital café and for Zura at his flat, where I kept Giorgi on the calls when Zura jumped ahead to the trip, and made sure the six call-log dates were written down exactly. The thing I learned this week: the self-dosing came out in silence in all three interviews, never from a question.
