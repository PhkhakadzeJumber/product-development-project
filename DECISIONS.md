**Problem we chose (06.10.2026, clarified 07.10.2026):** Patients with a regular doctor at a public clinic or hospital, especially those who live far from it, lose hours on a single visit: getting an appointment, travelling there and waiting to be seen, while their doctors work through more patients than the day has room for. Because the same patients must keep coming back, repeated visits add up to real money as well: transport, missed work, and paying for each visit and tests. Therefore, doctors are under huge pressure as well, having to treat so many patients, work tirelessly all day.
