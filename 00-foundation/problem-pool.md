# Problem Statement Shape

One statement, three lines. Three statements per member, all into `00-foundation/problem-pool.md` with the author's name on each.

```
AUTHOR: Jumber Pkhakadze
WHO I watched: [a specific, reachable person]
STRUGGLE I saw: [the behavior, when and where it happens]
COST I can name: [time, money, apologies, angry calls]
```

## The quality bar
| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| "students" | "third-year KIU students juggling three project courses at once" |
| "trouble coordinating" | "finds out at the deadline that two people built the same feature" |
| "sometimes" | "every Friday, when the weekend booking requests stack up" |

Three tests, apply all three:
1. **The room test.** Could a stranger pick your WHO out of a room from your description?
2. **The camera test.** Could you have filmed the STRUGGLE happening? If it only happens in your head, it fails.
3. **The desk test.** Could this statement have been written without leaving your desk? If yes, it fails. Personal observation is the whole assignment.

## Instant fails
- Starts with "an app that..." (a solution in a costume)
- A problem you read about online
- A person you could not actually interview next week
